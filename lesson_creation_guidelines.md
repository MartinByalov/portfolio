# Lesson Creation Guidelines

## Terminology and Styling
- **tone card**: A paragraph (or text block) that has an orange left border and a light blue/gray background (`#f3f7fa`). It is used to highlight specific text.
- **important card**: A callout box with a yellowish border and yellow background, used for important notes.
- **light border (лека граница)**: A light gray border around an element.
- **Dashes**: Do not use long dashes (`—`) in text. Always use normal dashes (`-`).

## Accordions
- **Main Points**: Do not add a tone (color label) or an icon to the title of a main point accordion (the top-level accordions outlining the lesson sections).

## General Design
- **Icons**: Do not put unnecessary icons (no decorative icons on component titles or paragraphs unless functionally necessary).
